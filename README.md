# INDX Audio Sound Pack

Free sounds and music for sound designers: 212 files, about 113 minutes, 24-bit 48 kHz WAV.

**Download:** open [Releases](../../releases/latest) and get `INDX-Audio-Sound-Pack-1.1.zip` (about 1.5 GB).

Inside: effects (kitchen, household, bathroom, sauna, vehicles, weather, animals, machines), loops, music, suspense and textures, and transitions such as reverse sweeps and risers. Each file has a short description in its metadata.

Read [LICENSE.txt](LICENSE.txt) for what you may and may not do. Free for your own projects, including commercial ones. Please do not redistribute the files as a library.

Made for sound designers. Works well with [SOUNDINDX](https://indx.audio).
